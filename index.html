About The Wendy House

A joyful haven where children lead the way.

Situated in Perth, our nursery welcomes children from as young as 3 months through to 5 years old. Our philosophy is simple: we create a safe, inclusive and home-like space, where children’s voices lead the way.

We weave environmental mindfulness into every aspect of nursery life — from our daily rituals to our engaging curriculum — so every child grows up with genuine heartfelt care and eco-friendly values at the core of everything we do.
