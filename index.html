About The Wendy House

Top choice for children in Perth.

Situated in the heart of Perth, our nursery welcomes children from 3 months through to 5 years old. Our philosophy is simple: we create a safe, inclusive and home-like space, where children’s voices lead the way.

  • Play-led learning
  • Expert team
  • Easy settling-in
  • Clean & safe
