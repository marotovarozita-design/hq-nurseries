At The Wendy House, we are family.

When your child joins us, they are embraced as if they are one of our own — because exceptional care begins with exceptional people.

Our story

More than a nursery — a magical haven in Perth.

Situated in the heart of Perth, The Wendy House Nursery is a vibrant, joyful haven where children are adored and encouraged to be unapologetically themselves. We welcome children from as young as 3 months through to 5 years old, sharing every giggle, celebrating every milestone and cherishing every moment along the way.

Our philosophy is simple: we create a safe, inclusive and home-like space, where children’s voices lead the way. By championing our devoted staff, supporting families and striving to brighten our community, we are dedicated to shaping brighter futures for all.

🏡
Our ethos

Genuine heartfelt care. Eco-friendly values.

We’re committed to protecting our planet, weaving environmental mindfulness into every aspect of nursery life — from our fun daily rituals to our engaging curriculum.

Guided by Realising the Ambition and the Curriculum for Excellence, we nurture a respect for the world around us, helping children develop an early understanding of sustainability and their place within it.

🌿
Our values

What we stand for

🤝 Known, not just cared for

Every child is known by name, by personality, by the tiny things that make them light up.

🎨 Voices that lead

We adapt rooms, routines and experiences to reflect the passions of the children in our care.

🌍 Rooted in community

From care-home visits to supporting local Perth businesses, our world is warm and well-connected.

🌱 Gentle on the planet

Recycling, nature exploration and a commitment to a net-zero future, lived every single day.

🪴 Happy staff, happy children

We invest in our team’s wellbeing because a nurturing environment begins with a supported team.

🧸 Home away from home

No uniforms. No rigid hierarchies. A working culture rooted in trust, respect and real connection.
