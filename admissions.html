Waiting list

Places are allocated with care

Demand for certain rooms can be high, particularly our preschool Eagles. We keep an active waiting list, allocating spaces fairly — prioritising siblings, continuity of care, and the age balance of each room.

Even if no immediate space is available, registering early means we can hold your place in the queue and give you plenty of notice when a session opens up.

Funded childcare

Up to 1,140 funded hours each year

We’re proud to offer funded Early Learning and Childcare (ELC) in partnership with Perth & Kinross Council, for eligible children aged 3–5 (and some eligible 2-year-olds).

Funding is provided by the Scottish Government and follows national guidance, including Realising the Ambition and Curriculum for Excellence.

Your child’s funded hours begin from the term after their 3rd birthday. Alongside this, our Strong Start programme offers additional support where needed — so every child and family feels included and able to thrive.

Tax-Free Childcare

Making childcare more affordable

For every £8 you pay into your childcare account, the government adds £2 — up to £2,000 per year per child (or £4,000 if your child has a disability).

Who can apply?

You may be eligible if:

  • You (and your partner, if you have one) are working
  • You earn at least the minimum required but less than £100,000 per year
  • Your child is under 12, or under 17 if they have a disability
How does it work?
  1. Set up an online childcare account at childcarechoices.gov.uk
  2. Pay into the account
  3. The government tops it up automatically
  4. You use the account to pay us directly
What can I use it for?

You can use Tax-Free Childcare to pay for nursery fees and any additional hours beyond your funded entitlement. Our team is always happy to help you combine funding, Tax-Free Childcare and private sessions.
