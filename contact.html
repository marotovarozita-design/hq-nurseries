Before you visit

A few things to know

When are you closed?

We’re closed for Easter Weekend and for two weeks over Christmas & New Year — mirroring Perth & Kinross Council so families with older children aren’t impacted.

That means The Wendy House is open for approximately 50 weeks each year.

How soon will I hear back?

We aim to reply to every enquiry within 2 working days. For urgent matters, please give us a call on 01738 632 885.

Can I bring my child on a visit?

Absolutely — we’d love to meet them. Visits are relaxed and we’ll give you a proper showround of our rooms, outdoor spaces, and answer any questions you have about starting with us.
