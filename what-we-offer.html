Our Playrooms

A room for every stage

Each room is lovingly designed by our staff to provide a stimulating, thoughtful and personal experience for every child.

🦅 Ages 3–5 · Preschool

Eagles — where curiosity takes flight

Our bright, green Eagle Room inspires curious, confident little learners. With natural wooden resources, a calm welcoming feel, and access to two large outdoor areas, our Eagles discover nature, build friendships and prepare for primary school with confidence.

Up to 30 children · 1 : 8 ratio. We are proud partners of Perth & Kinross Council, providing up to 1,140 hours of funded childcare.

🐝 Ages 2–3

Bumblebees — a warm start for busy little bees

A sunshine-filled space where 2–3 year olds feel safe, settled and truly cared for as they begin to find their independence. From sensory play to early communication, every moment supports emotional wellbeing as well as learning.

Funded childcare may be available for eligible 2-year-olds — our team is here to help you understand the criteria.

🦋 Ages 0–2

Butterflies — where tiny wings begin to flutter

A calm, soothing space in soft tones of light grey and beige. Every moment is guided by care, connection and trust. We follow each child’s individual routine, mirroring home comforts as closely as possible.

Parents provide meals and milk for 0–2s, keeping feeding consistent with your baby’s weaning journey.

🦉 Ages 0–2 · Opening mid-2026

Little Owls — a cosy nest to rest and grow

Our newest space — soft peach tones, a homely feel, and a thoughtful design for our youngest children. Currently being carefully prepared and welcoming early interest now.

Now taking early interest
Curriculum

Strong foundations. Bright futures.

Our curriculum is purposefully designed to give children the knowledge and confidence to shape their future. Through a balance of child-led exploration and carefully planned experiences, we introduce a wide range of learning opportunities.

  • Early Literacy and Numeracy
  • Sensory Exploration
  • Science and Discovery
  • Creative Expression
  • Physical Development

Guided by Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence and Realising the Ambition, we follow each child’s interests and natural curiosity, creating learning that feels exciting, engaging and truly relevant.

A typical day

Daily routine at The Wendy House

8:30 – 9:30

Warm welcomes & free play

Children arrive at their own pace, greeted by their key person and settling into their favourite corners.

9:30 – 10:00

Morning snack & a sing

A nutritious snack together — staff eating alongside children and modelling relaxed, social mealtimes.

10:00 – 11:30

Play & purposeful learning

Child-led exploration alongside planned experiences — literacy, numeracy, creativity and outdoor adventures.

11:30 – 12:30

Nutritious lunch

Fresh, balanced meals prepared by our chef Andzelika at Catering Twist — mindful of every dietary need.

12:30 – 14:30

Rest, read & reflect

Quiet time, story corners, naps for those who need them, and gentle activities for those who don’t.

14:30 – 16:00

Outdoor adventures

Garden play, nature walks, litter picks and community visits — the world beyond our walls.

16:00 – 16:30

Afternoon snack

A little top-up before the final stretch of play and discovery.

16:30 – 17:30

Wind-down & goodbyes

Calm, child-led activities. Key person chats with parents, sharing the highlights of your child’s day.

Food & snack

Fresh, balanced, full of flavour

Throughout the day, children enjoy a morning snack, a nutritious lunch and an afternoon snack — well-fuelled and ready to learn and play.

Our approach is guided by the Scottish Government’s Setting the Table and NHS nutritional guidance. Here’s a message from our Chef Andzelika at Catering Twist:

“I’m really passionate about creating fresh, balanced and nutritious food that supports children’s growth and wellbeing, while still being full of flavour and fun.”

Your child

Every detail, centred on your little one

Settling in at The Wendy House

We offer complimentary settling-in sessions — giving both you and your child time to experience our setting, build trust, and begin forming meaningful connections with our team. Every child settles in their own way.

From the very beginning you can expect regular updates and open communication, a dedicated key person, and gentle, well-supported transitions between rooms — with extra reassurance for children (and parents) who may need it.

Safeguarding and security

Your child’s safety is at the heart of everything we do. All visitors are carefully ID-checked and never left unsupervised, and we will only ever release children to a parent or authorised guardian. A vigilant, highly trained team and clear safeguarding practices mean you can feel confident your child is safe with us — every moment of every day.

Happy children start with happy staff

A nurturing environment for children begins with a happy, supported team. We invest in our staff’s wellbeing, creating a positive, caring culture that children feel every day.

A place for families

We want every parent to feel welcome, supported and part of our community from the very beginning. Through strong, trusting relationships, Stay-and-Play sessions, milestone celebrations and a confidential listening ear whenever you need it.

Caring for our world

We encourage children to care for the environment through everyday experiences — recycling, nature walks, litter picks and small acts of stewardship. We’re also committed to meaningful steps towards a more sustainable, net-zero future.
